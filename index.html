Simple, useful, and yours.

NibbleNote is an open-source Android recipe organiser built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. It is designed to keep everyday cooking ideas easy to find, with local-first storage and no account to create.

Recipe imports depend on what a website makes available and whether it allows automated access. Check imported quantities and instructions before cooking, especially where allergies or food safety are involved.
